Merchant Application Preparation Checklist

Fintech providers require strict documentation. We ensure your application is comprehensive.

✓
1. Certified Corporate Legal Documents Certificate of Incorporation, Articles of Association / Operating Agreement, and IRS EIN letter (CP575 or 147C).
✓
2. Director & Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO) KYC Valid government passport copy and proof of residential address (utility bill, government letter, or personal bank statement).
✓
3. Live Commercial Website Readiness Payment gateways mandate a functioning website with clear product descriptions, prices, company registration details, Terms of Service, Refund Policy, and contact information.
✓
4. Business Operating Evidence & Supplier Invoices Where applicable, supplier agreements, contracts, sample invoices, or proof of inventory to verify your commercial activity.
